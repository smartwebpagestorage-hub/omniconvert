1. 100% Client-Side Privacy Guarantee

OmniConvert Studio is built with extreme privacy in mind. All document conversions (including PDF compression, merging, image processing, OCR, barcode, and receipt generation) execute 100% locally in your web browser using client-side JavaScript. We do NOT upload, inspect, store, or transmit your files to any remote server or third-party cloud service.

2. Data Collection and Usage

We do not collect personal identification information. We do not require account registration, email addresses, or phone numbers to use our tools. Your selected files exist only in your browser's temporary memory (RAM) and are discarded as soon as you close or refresh the page.

The application stores small preferences (such as your chosen theme - light/dark mode, and interface language - Hindi/English) locally on your device via browser localStorage for your convenience.
