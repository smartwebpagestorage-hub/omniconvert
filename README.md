## ✨ Key Features & Included Tools

### 1. 🗜️ PDF Compression & Optimization
- **Smart Target Size Compression:** Compress PDFs to exact file size limits (**500 KB, 1 MB, 2 MB, 4 MB, or Custom MB**) with high visual clarity.
- **DPI Scaling:** Choose between 1x (Fast), 2x (HD 150 DPI), and 3x (Print 300 DPI) rendering.

### 2. 🔄 Conversion Tools
- **PDF ➔ Image:** Convert PDF pages into high-resolution PNG, JPEG, or WebP (single pages or bulk ZIP).
- **Image ➔ PDF:** Merge multiple images into organized PDF documents with customizable page margins and orientation.
- **Text ➔ PDF:** Professional Hindi (Devanagari) & English document creator with live pagination.
- **PDF ➔ Text:** Instant text extraction with keyword search.
- **Image ➔ Text (OCR):** 100% client-side optical character recognition.
- **Image Converter & Splitter:** Convert image formats or slice images into 2×2, 3×3 grids.

### 3. 📑 PDF Organization
- **Merge & Combine:** Storyboard drag-and-drop merging of PDFs and images.
- **Split & Extract:** Split multi-page PDFs or extract custom page ranges (e.g. `1-3, 5, 8-10`).
- **Rotate & Delete:** Visual thumbnail selector to rotate pages (90°/180°) or remove unwanted pages.

### 4. ✍️ Editing & Security
- **Digital Signatures:** Touch, mouse, or typed digital signatures stamped anywhere on the document.
- **Redaction & Annotation:** Blackout confidential numbers, highlight text, and draw freehand notes.
- **Watermarking & Page Numbering:** Add custom diagonal watermarks and automated `Page X of Y` footers.
- **Password Protection:** Encrypt or unlock protected PDFs with AES encryption.

### 5. 🧰 Office & Administrative Power Tools
- **📷 Passport Photo Studio:** Generate standard 3.5×4.5 cm passport photo sheets (**8, 16, 24, 32 prints**) on A4 paper with cutting guides.
- **🪪 ID Card Merger:** Merge Front and Back scans of Aadhaar, PAN, or Voter ID cards onto a single A4 sheet.
- **📱 QR Code & Barcode Studio:** Generate offline vector QR codes and Code 39 barcodes with instant PDF slips.
- **📊 CSV / Excel to PDF Table:** Transform raw CSV or spreadsheet data into striped, multi-page administrative PDF reports.
- **🧾 Official Receipt & Challan Maker:** Generate itemized payment receipts with auto-numbering, INR totals, and official RO Faridabad seal.
- **🏷️ Bulk File Renamer:** Batch rename dozens of files with prefixes, suffixes, date stamps, and sequential numbers.
- **🛡️ Metadata & Privacy Cleaner:** Deep inspect and strip hidden EXIF camera tags, GPS coordinates, and author metadata.

## 🔒 100% Privacy Guarantee

OmniConvert Studio processes all files **strictly inside your web browser** using HTML5 and WebAssembly APIs. 
- **Zero Server Uploads:** Your sensitive office and personal files never leave your computer.
- **Offline Capable:** Works without an active internet connection.
